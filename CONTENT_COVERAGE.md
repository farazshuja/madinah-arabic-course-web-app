# Book 1 content coverage

Source: `../books/Book1_Key.pdf`, a 54-page scanned Key. Explanations were adapted from visual reading of the source. The website covers all 23 lessons, plus Lesson 4a, with 143 cards. Standalone vocabulary lists and main-book exercises/solutions are outside the current concepts-only scope; vocabulary entries containing grammar notes are included.

## Source coverage

- Lessons 1–12: pages 3–30, including 4a and both parts of Lesson 9.
- Lesson 13: pages 31–36, Parts A, B and C; sound and broken plurals, every pattern in the table, plural pronouns/demonstratives, verb endings and irregular forms.
- Lesson 14: pages 37–39, points 1–7, including the short-name exception and changing endings of “which”.
- Lesson 15: pages 40–41, points 1–5.
- Lesson 16: pages 42–43, points 1–2 and the basic-rule qualification.
- Lesson 17: page 44, explicitly labelled as revision with its two alternative plurals.
- Lesson 18: pages 45–46, points 1–2, accusative spelling note and vocabulary footnote.
- Lessons 19–20: pages 47–50, numbers one/two and three–ten, case/gender rules and omitted currency unit.
- Lesson 21: page 51, explicitly labelled as revision with its demonstrative and verb vocabulary.
- Lesson 22: pages 52–53, contextual omission of tanwin, all eight listed groups (including all four plural subgroups), doctors-pattern note.
- Lesson 23: page 54, ordinary majrur review and the special ending of the illustrated diptote names.

The introductory reading-symbol glossary on page 2 is not a numbered lesson. Arabic examples retain vowel marks. Cards cite lesson, source point/part and printed page. Split cards preserve the source point in their citation. Repeated concepts share tags and remain in book order.
